Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

How our tools work

Many tools on SmartTools Hub process your input in your own browser. For example, text calculations, password generation and image resizing are designed to run locally, without sending their content to our own server.

Third-party services

The QR code generator opens an external QR service when you request a QR code. Text entered into that tool is included in the request to that service. Avoid using it for private information.

Our hosting provider may collect technical logs, such as IP addresses, under its own policies. Links to external sites are governed by those sites' privacy policies.

Local storage

We save your light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. You can clear it through your browser settings.

Advertising and analytics

This version of the site does not intentionally include advertising or analytics scripts. If we add them later, we will update this policy to describe their use and any applicable choices.
